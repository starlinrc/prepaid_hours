Prepaid Hours / Service Packages

Sell prepaid hour packages and track consumption via timesheets

Allows selling prepaid service packages (hours or credits). Automatically tracks consumption from validated timesheets. Alerts customer when balance is low or expired.

Key Features

  • Sell hour/credit packages as normal sale order products
  • Balance created automatically on sale confirmation
  • Automatic consumption tracking via timesheets
  • Email alerts when balance is low or exhausted
  • Manual adjustment wizard with full audit trail
  • Compatible with Odoo 17, 18 and 19
